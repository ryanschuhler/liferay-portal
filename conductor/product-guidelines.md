# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
- Professional, clear, and direct.
- Use active voice and avoid jargon when possible.
- Consistent terminology (e.g., always "module" instead of "plugin" for OSGi components).

## UX Principles
- Clarity over complexity: Design interfaces that are intuitive.
- Consistency: Maintain a uniform look and feel across all modules.
- Accessibility: Ensure compliance with WCAG standards.
- Performance: Prioritize fast load times and smooth interactions.

## Branding
- Use Liferay's official color palette and typography.
- Consistent logo usage and placement.

## Component Design
- Reuse existing Lexicon/Clay components to ensure UI consistency.
- Modular design: Components should be self-contained and reusable.
